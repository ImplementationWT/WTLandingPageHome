FAQ

Questions we get
every first call.

Do I need to already have monday.com?
+
No. We build from scratch or work with an existing account. Starting fresh? We'll recommend the right plan for your team size — and the architecture comes the same either way.
Do you build custom software, or only monday.com?
+
Both. monday.com is the backbone, but when your operation needs more we build around it — web apps (Next.js / TypeScript), databases, mobile apps, and custom integrations across your stack. The architecture stays one connected system.
Why do you insist on building the architecture first?
+
Because automation on top of messy data is just faster chaos — it's the single most common reason implementations fail. We structure your data first, so everything built on top of it holds, and scales, instead of collapsing the moment you grow.
How long does a full implementation take?
+
Most projects go live in 4–6 weeks. More complex scopes — multiple departments or custom integrations — run 8–12 weeks. We agree on the timeline before we start. No surprises.
How does the phased model work?
+
One path, three phases, taken in order. Phase 1 ($2,000, fixed) builds your full data architecture live and usable. Phase 2 ($3k–$10k+) adds automation and the AI layer — priced one automation at a time, against a structure you've already validated, so you go at your own pace. Phase 3 is ongoing support, reactive at $0/mo, with an optional retainer if you want dedicated hours. You only ever commit to the next phase once the last one is real in front of you.
Is this a one-time cost or a subscription?
+
It's a phased investment, not a subscription. You pay a fixed $2,000 for the structure, then price automation and AI piece by piece as you go ($3k–$10k+ depending on scope). After that, ongoing support is optional and starts reactive at $0/mo — you only pay when you actually need us. You own the system the whole way through.
What if my team doesn't adopt the system?
+
Adoption is part of the build. We design every system around how your team actually works — not how they're supposed to — and we don't hand off until it runs without us. Training is included, plus recorded walkthroughs your team can revisit anytime.
What happens after launch — do you just disappear?
+
No. We stabilize the system live and don't hand off until your team actually runs it without us. And if you like how we work, you have the option of ongoing support — a team that already knows your setup, on call as you grow.
What's included in Ongoing Support?
+
Dedicated hours every month for fixes, new requests, and improvements. Monthly system reviews. Priority response. And a team that already knows your setup inside out — so no context-switching every time you need something.
Are software licenses included?
+
No — and that's in your favor. Your monday.com plan and any tools in your stack (Zapier, Make, n8n, Slack, Google Workspace) are billed directly to you, so you own them and pay platform cost with no markup. We recommend exactly what you need, nothing you don't.
Can I start small and scale up later?
+
That's the whole idea. You start with Phase 1 — the fixed $2,000 structure — and see it working before committing to anything else. From there you add automation one piece at a time, at whatever pace fits your budget. Start where you're comfortable; scale when the system proves itself.
Can I adjust the scope after we start?
+
Yes. If your needs grow, we scope the adjustment and apply a credit toward the new engagement. We work with you, not against you — the scope we agree on is contractually guaranteed.
Do you work with companies outside the US?
+
Yes — we've built systems for clients in 15+ countries across 5 continents. We're a registered U.S. company (Whatever Tech LLC, Florida), so you contract with confidence. All sessions are remote; we adapt to your timezone and language.
