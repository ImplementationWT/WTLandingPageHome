What's an app

An app is the screen one team works in, connected to all your data.

Every business runs on the same things: clients, orders, inventory, invoices, people. We store each one once, in databases, and connect them all. Then each team gets its app: a screen with only what it needs to do its job, on desktop and mobile.

01Your databases.

Clients, orders, invoices and everything your business tracks, stored once and connected to each other.

02Your apps.

One per team: the screen your people open every day, with only what they need.

03All connected.

When sales closes a deal, logistics sees it. When logistics dispatches, admin bills it. Nobody retypes anything.

    What is an orchestrator?
    +
    The piece that moves information between your systems on its own. When something happens in one place, like a payment coming in or someone filling out a form, the orchestrator runs the next steps in the others: it updates records, sends messages and asks the AI to decide when a decision is needed. It's what makes your apps, your tools and the AI work as one system.

    +
